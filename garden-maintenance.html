Good to know

Regular visits are the easiest way to keep a garden under control. Lawns stay thicker, hedges keep their shape and weeds never get the chance to take over. You don't need a big contract either. Visits can be every week or two in the growing season, or just a few times a year for hedges and a tidy. In autumn I also do leaf clearance, and those slots go quickly, so it's worth booking early.
