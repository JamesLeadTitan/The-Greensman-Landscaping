What the Work Covers

Most decks that go wrong were built on a poor frame. I spend the time getting the frame level and solid, because everything else sits on it.

  • Clearing and preparing the ground
  • Weed membrane under the deck
  • A level, solid frame
  • Laying the deck boards
  • Steps and edging
  • Clearing away the waste

  5. Clear-upI take the offcuts and waste away.

Good to know

Decking needs air underneath it and a bit of a fall so rain runs off the boards. Without that, boards stay wet, go green and get slippery. I build with that in mind from the start. If you already have a deck that's slippery or tired, a proper clean can make a big difference before you think about replacing it.
