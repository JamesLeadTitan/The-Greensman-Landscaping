What the Work Covers

A patio only lasts if the base under it is right. That's where most of the work goes, and it's the part you never see once it's finished.

  • Digging out and levelling
  • A compacted sub-base
  • Laying and pointing the slabs
  • A fall so rainwater runs off
  • Paths and steps to match
  • Edging and making good the lawn
The Process

What to Expect

  1. Look at the jobI come out, measure up and check the ground and drainage.
  2. Free quoteYou get a clear quote covering the full job, with no call-out fee.
  3. GroundworkI dig out, level and lay a firm sub-base. Rushing this is why patios sink.
  4. LayingSlabs go down level with the right fall, then the joints are pointed.
  5. Clear-upI take the waste away and leave the garden tidy.

Good to know

Most patio problems come from what's underneath: no proper sub-base, or slabs laid without a fall so water sits on them. If yours is rocking or holding puddles, the base is usually the cause, and I'll tell you honestly whether it can be lifted and relaid or needs starting again.
