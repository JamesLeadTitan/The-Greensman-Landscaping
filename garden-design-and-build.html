What You'll Pay and How I Quote

A full garden is priced on the real job: the size, the levels, the access and the materials. That's why I won't guess over the phone. I walk the garden with you, measure up and look at the levels, access and drainage, then send a written, itemised quote. Fixed, not estimated.

You get a written programme with the quote, so you know when each part happens before you commit to anything.

The Process

What to Expect

  1. Site visitI walk the garden with you, measure up and look at levels, access and drainage.
  2. Fixed written quoteAn itemised quote and a programme, so you know the price and the plan.
  3. ProgrammeA start date and a week-by-week plan. Materials are ordered and on site before day one, so the work doesn't stall halfway.
  4. BuildRegular updates, a tidy site every evening, and no stage paid for until you're happy it's done.
  5. Handover and careA walk-round, planting notes and a written guarantee, with ongoing care if you want it.

Good to know

The part nobody photographs decides how a garden lasts: where the water goes, how the ground is held and how machines get in and out without damaging what stays. I sort that first, then the terrace, lawn and planting go on top. Retaining walls, garden rooms, driveway drainage and boundary heights can all bring planning or building regulations requirements. I flag anything relevant at quote stage and handle it where needed. A full redesign and build usually takes four to ten weeks, depending on the size of the garden and what's involved.
