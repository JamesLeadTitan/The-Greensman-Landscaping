What the Work Covers

Whether it's real turf or artificial grass, the result comes down to the ground underneath. Lumps and soft spots show through either way.

  • Lifting the old lawn
  • Levelling the ground
  • Preparing the soil or sub-base
  • Laying turf or artificial grass

Good to know

With real turf, timing matters. Spring and autumn are the easiest times for it to root, and new turf needs regular watering for the first few weeks. Artificial grass can go down most of the year, but it needs a firm, well-drained base or it ends up lumpy and holds water. Either way, the preparation is where the money goes, and it's the part that decides how the lawn looks in a year's time.
