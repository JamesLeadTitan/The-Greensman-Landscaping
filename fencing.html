What the Work Covers

A fence is only as good as its posts. If they're not set deep and solid, the first windy winter shows it.

  • Taking down and clearing old fencing
  • Setting new posts
  • Putting up new panels or boards
  • Replacing broken panels and posts
  • Gravel boards along the bottom
  • Taking the old fence away
The Process

What to Expect

  1. Look at the jobI measure the run and check the posts, the ground and anything in the way.
  2. Free quoteA clear quote for the whole job, with no call-out fee.
  3. Out with the oldOld panels and posts come down and get taken away.
  4. Posts and panelsNew posts go in solid, then the panels or boards go up straight.
  5. Clear-upI tidy up both sides and take the waste away.

Good to know

If only one or two panels have blown out, it's usually worth checking the posts before replacing anything. Rotten posts are the most common reason a fence leans or a new panel won't sit right. I'll look at the whole run and tell you what actually needs doing, not sell you a new fence you don't need.
