What the Work Covers

A renovation usually starts with a proper clearance. Once I can see what's there, it's easier to decide what stays, what goes and what gets put back.

  • Clearing overgrown beds and borders
  • Cutting back and pruning
  • Weeding and digging over
  • Mulching beds
  • Garden waste removal
  • New turf, paths or patio if you want them
The Process

What to Expect

  1. Walk roundWe go round the garden together and agree what you want.
  2. Free quoteA clear quote with no call-out fee.
  3. ClearanceI clear the overgrowth and take the waste away.
  4. Putting it backBeds are dug over and mulched, and any new turf or paving goes in.
  5. Keeping it that wayIf you want, I can come back regularly so it doesn't get away from you again.

Good to know

Clearances often turn up more than you expect: old paving under the weeds, buried rubble, roots from plants long gone. I clear it properly, so you're left with ground you can actually use. Once it's clear, you can decide what you want next. That might be new beds, a lawn, a patio or just keeping it tidy. I can do any of those, or simply hand it back to you clean.
