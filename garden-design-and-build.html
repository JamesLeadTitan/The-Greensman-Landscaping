The Process

What to Expect

  1. Site visitI walk the garden with you, measure up and check the levels, access and drainage.
  2. Written quoteYou get a quote with everything listed. The price is fixed.
  3. Start dateWe agree a start date and I give you a plan of what happens each week. Materials are ordered and on site before I start.
  4. The workI keep you updated, tidy up at the end of every day, and you don't pay for a stage until you're happy with it.
  5. HandoverWe walk round it together when it's done. You get planting notes and a written guarantee, and I can keep looking after the garden if you want.

Good to know

Most of what makes a garden last is underneath: the drainage, the levels and a solid base. I get that right first, then the patio, lawn and planting go on top. Retaining walls, garden rooms, driveway drainage and fence or wall heights can need planning permission or building regs. I'll tell you at quote stage if anything does, and sort it. A full garden usually takes four to ten weeks, depending on the size and what's involved.
